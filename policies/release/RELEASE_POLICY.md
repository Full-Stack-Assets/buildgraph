# Release and External-Effect Policy

## Rule

Generated or agent-assisted artifacts are candidates, not final records. A release or external effect may occur only after independent validation, a named accountable human approval, and the native system’s required confirmation/control path.

## Prohibited in BuildGraph v0

- Merging to protected branches.
- Production deployment or infrastructure mutation.
- Sending external email, invitations, direct messages, posts, or announcements.
- Publishing content or media.
- Payment, refund, purchase, transfer, credit, accounting, tax, or billing action.
- Contract signature, legal assertion, license grant, or formal submission.
- Identity, permission, access-policy, or employment action.
- Deletion, overwrite, master-label change, or irreversible data operation.

## Required release evidence

A future release-capable workflow must provide a validated release package, an independent quality gate result, complete provenance, named human authority, rollback/recovery evidence where applicable, and a native execution receipt. Missing evidence blocks the release request.
